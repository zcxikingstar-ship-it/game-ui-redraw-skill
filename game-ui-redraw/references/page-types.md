# Page Types

Use this reference after inspecting the screenshot when the page type is recognizable. Do not infer pages or states that are not visible unless the user explicitly asks for extensions.

## Lobby

Prioritize the full background, main call-to-action buttons, top resource bar, player info area, feature-entry icons, bottom navigation, notification badges, and persistent decorative frames. Treat character art, weapons, buildings, and large scene objects as background unless the user asks to isolate them.

## Event

Prioritize the event title plate, hero panel, reward slots, progress or milestone tracks, countdown area, rules entry, purchase or claim buttons, rarity frames, and promotional decorations. Mark countdowns, quantities, prices, and reward names as text roles instead of baking them into art.

## Shop Or Gift Pack

Prioritize product cards, price areas, discount ribbons, currency icons, bonus labels, purchase buttons, selection frames, and sold-out or limit markers. Keep prices and quantities as text metadata. Do not invent coupons, currencies, or payment states that are not visible.

## Popup

Prioritize the modal backdrop, title frame, content panel, close button, confirm and cancel buttons, reward display areas, separators, and corner decorations. Extract buttons and panels separately when they can be reused.

## Battle HUD

Prioritize health or energy bars, skill buttons, joystick areas, minimaps, status icons, target markers, cooldown frames, and damage or score text areas. Keep dynamic combat values as text metadata. Do not isolate gameplay characters or environment props by default.
